    AI Scam Detector Technical & Architectural Overview

    Q: What is the core definition of AI Scam Detector?
    "An AI-powered safety assistant that analyzes messages, website URLs, and screenshots to detect potential scams, explain warning signs, and guide users toward safer actions."
    Q: Where is AI used in the architecture?
    "AI is used in the NLP and machine-learning component (TF-IDF vectorizer + Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, Linear SVM) to learn statistical n-gram patterns from labeled scam and legitimate corpora, combined with an explainable rule engine and URL entropy analyzer."
    Q: What makes this project different from a standard spam filter?
    "A basic spam detector only performs binary classification (Spam/Not Spam). This project combines multi-modal input (text, URLs, and OCR screenshots), explains specific warning signs (urgency, credential harvesting, TLD abuse, brand spoofing), categorizes the scam into 7 threat classes, and delivers actionable safety recommendations."
